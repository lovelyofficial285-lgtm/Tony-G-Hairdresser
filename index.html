01 · The Practitioner

Part stylist. Part therapist.
Entirely hair doctor.

Tony G has spent over twenty-five years behind the chair — long enough to earn the nickname her clients gave her: the Hair Doctor. Her diagnosis-first philosophy treats hair the way a good clinician treats a patient: listen carefully, treat precisely, then teach you how to stay well.

She is also an Intuitive Therapist, working to a simple Hair–Body–Mind principle: when hair is genuinely healthy — soft, manageable, shaped to hold for months — it quietly changes how you carry yourself. Two days a week, by appointment only: unhurried, exact, and in high demand for a reason.
